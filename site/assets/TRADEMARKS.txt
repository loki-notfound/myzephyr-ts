# The lateo name and marks

The code and reusable material in this repository are licensed under the

are not: they identify the lateo projects and their operator, and all rights
in them are reserved.

## What is a mark
- The name "lateo" and the hostnames under lateo.dev.
- `assets/logo/`: the mark, the wordmark and every lockup.
- `assets/products/`: the product icons, favicons, web app manifests and Open
  Graph images.
- `assets/github/`: the avatar, the social preview and the README banners.
- `assets/desktop/`: the tray icons and the installer artwork.

Everything else in this repository is under the BSD 3-Clause License, with the
third-party sources recorded in ASSETS.md keeping their own licences (IBM Plex
Mono outlines: SIL Open Font License 1.1; Natural Earth map data: public
domain).

## What you may do
- Keep the mark files unchanged in a copy or fork of a lateo repository.
- Use the name and the marks to refer to the lateo projects truthfully, for
  example in an article or a list of nodes or wallets.

## What you may not do
- Present your own site, app, build or service as lateo, or as run, endorsed or
  approved by lateo, by using the name or the marks.
- Use the marks, or anything confusingly similar, as the name, logo or icon of
  another product, in particular a wallet, a node or an explorer.
- Distribute a modified wallet or app under the lateo name or icons. A fork
  takes its own name and its own icons.

Why: people must be able to trust that something carrying the lateo name and
marks comes from lateo. For software that handles keys and funds this matters.

## Carrying marks into other repositories
A repository that takes over mark files (for example the favicon and logo of a
site) states in its README or licence notes that those files fall outside its
own licence and under this text.
